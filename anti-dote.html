The Bar

Come for the coffee,
stay for the community.

Anti Dote is a coffee bar that takes its cues from experimental cuisine. The roster rotates, the preparations are not always the obvious ones, and nothing on the counter is there because every other café has it.

It sits downstairs from two working studios, which means the room fills with people between takes — crews on a break, dancers before a class, and anyone who came in for a flat white and stayed for the argument about it.
