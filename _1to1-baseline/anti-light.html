Anti Light by Anti Labs Studios offers a versatile space for photographers, videographers, brands, and content creators.

Our studio space includes a cyclorama, chic makeup station, lounge, customisable backdrops and a range of props. We offer various backdrops and top-notch studio equipment for all experience levels.

Located on New BEL Road, a 7 minute walk from MS Ramaiah University, with hues of grey, whites, and blacks, it is the perfect creative space for photo shoots, video shoots, E-commerce, and podcasts.
