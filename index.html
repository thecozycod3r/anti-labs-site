The Spaces

  • A controlled-light sanctuary for visual storytelling. Cyclorama wall, modular lighting rigs, seamless backdrops, and a prop room — everything you need to shoot editorial, commercial, or personal work at a professional level without the agency price tag.

  • Sprung hardwood floors, mirrored walls, aerial rigging points, and a pole — designed for every discipline that demands space, sweat, and surrender. Whether you're drilling a choreography or teaching a sunrise Pilates class, Anti Motion holds the floor for you.

  • The antidote to generic café culture. Anti Dote is a coffee bar that takes its cues from experimental cuisine — rotating single-origins, unexpected preparations, and a menu designed to fuel creative sessions without the crash. Come for the coffee, stay for the community.
